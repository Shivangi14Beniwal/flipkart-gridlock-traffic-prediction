── PROBLEM ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Predict normalised traffic demand (0–1) for 41,778 (geohash, timestamp)
pairs on Day 49 (test), given Day 48 full data + partial Day 49 training.
Evaluation metric: Spearman rank correlation × 100 (approx).

── DATA INSIGHTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Train: Day 48 (69,427 rows, all 96 × 15-min slots) +
         Day 49 (7,872 rows, slots 0–8, i.e. 00:00–02:00 only)
• Test:  Day 49, slots 9–55 (02:15–13:45) — daytime
• Geohashes: 6-char (~75 m × 150 m cells), compact geographic area
• "RoadType" / "NumberofLanes" etc. are TIME-VARYING per geohash —
  they reflect observed traffic state, not static road properties.
• Day 48 has exactly one row per (geohash, slot) → geo_slot_mean48
  equals the raw Day-48 demand at that exact location + time.
  This is the "golden feature": a direct 1-day lag.

── APPROACH ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Step 1: Feature Engineering (35 features)

  Temporal
    hour, slot (0–95), sin/cos encoding, cyclic hour encoding

  Geohash aggregates (from Day-48 reference data)
    geo_mean, geo_std, geo_median, geo_max, geo_q75
    geo_smooth  — Bayesian smoothed mean (alpha=30)

  Primary lag feature  ← most important
    geo_slot_mean   = Day-48 demand at exact (geohash, slot)  [direct lookup]
    geo_slot_smooth = Bayesian-smoothed version (alpha=15)
    geo_hour_mean   = (geohash, hour) mean as first fallback
    lag1            = geo_slot → geo_hour → geo_mean → global slot mean

  Road-type aggregates
    road_mean, road_slot_mean
    roadlanes_mean, roadlanes_slot_mean

  Spatial prefix aggregates (geohash neighbourhood)
    p4_mean, p4_slot  (first 4 chars ≈ ~2.4 km² zone)
    p5_mean, p5_slot  (first 5 chars ≈ ~0.6 km² zone)

  Interactions
    geo_x_slot_ratio, lag1_x_road, geo_x_lanes, smooth_x_road

Step 2: Validation
  OOF within Day 48 (first-half ↔ second-half slot swap) to avoid
  within-sample leakage when computing geohash aggregate features.
  Secondary val: Day-49 train (night slots, Spearman ~0.80).
  Note: night val is a lower bound; test is daytime → LB ~0.907+.

Step 3: Two-Configuration Ensemble
  Config A: LGB (0.70) + CB (0.30)  → matches A10, LB 90.31
  Config B: LGB (0.75) + CB (0.25)  → matches A13, LB 90.73
  Final   : 0.60 × Config_A + 0.40 × Config_B = 90.73638

  Both configs use the same 35 features; the blend adds diversity.
  LightGBM: lr=0.015, depth=8, num_leaves=200, early_stopping=80
  CatBoost:  lr=0.05,  depth=7, l2=3,          early_stopping=50
  Final models retrained on full data (Day 48 + Day 49 train).

── KEY LEARNING ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The jump from 88.5 → 90.7 came entirely from ONE insight:
  geo_slot_mean48[geohash, slot] IS the Day-48 demand at that exact
  location and time. Using it directly as a feature (rather than as
  a component of a geohash label encoding) gave the model a
  near-perfect 1-day lag without any temporal leakage.

What DID NOT work
  • Power transforms on demand target
  • Post-hoc calibration / scaling
  • Spatial geohash decoding (KDTree neighbors)
  • RoadType-change correction (A10 already handles it implicitly)
  • Extrapolating day-over-day demand ratios to daytime slots
  • Additional model types (XGB adds noise vs LGB+CB)

Theoretical ceiling with available data: ~91–92.
Scores above 92 likely require external geographic / POI data.
